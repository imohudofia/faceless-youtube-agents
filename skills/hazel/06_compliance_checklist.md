# 06 — Compliance Checklist (run before every manual upload)

_Frugality/household = low risk, but still protect the channel. ~3 min. AI disclosure is handled by YOU in YouTube Studio at upload (NOT in the video, NOT here)._

## A. Claims
- [ ] No medical claims / no "cure/heal/treat". Frame health-adjacent tips (foot soak, etc.) as common home remedies with a light "be careful / patch test" caution.
- [ ] No invented studies, statistics, experts.
- [ ] No false attacks on specific brands ("Brand X is dangerous"). General "save money vs pricey sprays" framing is fine.
- [ ] Tips are common, verifiable, general knowledge.

## B. Anti-slop / authenticity
- [ ] Script original (not copied from reference videos).
- [ ] Real visual progression — many distinct shots, varied motion (not one static image).
- [ ] Hazel variant-switching applied (no single frozen image reused throughout).
- [ ] Voice natural (hazel_2), not robotic.
- [ ] Thumbnail style varied over time (don't reuse identical template forever).

## C. File QC (before upload)
- [ ] Title matches content (no clickbait it doesn't deliver).
- [ ] Thumbnail readable at small size; text crisp (added in code, not AI-rendered).
- [ ] Captions present + synced.
- [ ] Audio: voice clear, music quiet.
- [ ] Description has: 1-line hook + (optional) friendly disclaimer like "These are general money-saving tips that work for me — use your own judgement."

## D. The one rule
If unsure whether a claim is safe/true → soften or cut it. Reach is worthless if the channel gets flagged.

## Note on AI disclosure
- You decide whether to tick YouTube Studio's "altered/synthetic content" box at upload. Nothing about AI goes inside the video itself.
