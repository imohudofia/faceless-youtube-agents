# 08 — Captions, Pacing & Personal Voice (PROVEN — apply to every video)

_Locked after testing on the vinegar video. Supports (does not contradict) 02_content_format.md and 05_assembly_motion_rules.md._

---

## CAPTIONS (burned in, in code — never inside the AI images)
- Method: single-pass FFmpeg `subtitles` burn-in AFTER assembly, synced to the hazel_2 voiceover.
- **Max 2 lines. Max ~6 words per line (~9 words total on screen at once).**
- Captions display in SHORT PHRASE CHUNKS that change as she speaks — NOT a whole sentence at once. Break at natural pauses.
- Style (approved): white bold text, solid black outline, highly transparent dark box (use ASS `BackColour=&HE0000000`), lower-center, LARGE font (readable for 50+ viewers and mobile).
- No emoji. No text inside generated images (style anchor already enforces "no text").

## PACING (visual rhythm)
- A visual change roughly **every 6–9 seconds**. No single visual on screen longer than ~10 seconds.
- **SMART AUDIO PACING:** Only add breathing pauses (`0.6s` audio padding) to clips where the narration text ends with a period, question mark, or exclamation point (`.?!`). Do NOT pad clips that break mid-sentence, to ensure natural flow.
- Achieve variety by alternating B-ROLL <-> HAZEL shots within long beats (Hazel shots are free library reuse — don't generate new images just for pacing).
- Motion stays GENTLE/SLOW (calm 50+ audience): small zoom range (~1.0–1.12), slow pans. No fast/jarring moves. Cycle motions so no two consecutive slides match.
- Note: over-editing/flashy cuts REDUCE retention with older audiences — keep it calm and clean.

## PERSONAL VOICE (the relatability "secret sauce")
- Weave in **3–4 brief first-person memory / lived-experience touches per video**, in Hazel's warm voice. NOT on every item (that feels forced) — sprinkle naturally.
- Flavor (write fresh per video, fit the topic): "my mother used to do this with every towel," "back when my kids were small and money was tight," "I learned this the hard way after ruining a good blouse," "a neighbor taught me this years ago."
- This lived-experience warmth is what separates a relatable-peer channel from generic AI slop. It is a core part of Hazel's brand voice.
- Keep all claims non-medical, truthful, no false brand attacks.
- IMPORTANT: any `[Personal Touch: ...]` style markers in a draft are review labels only — the voiceover speaks ONLY the sentence, never the bracket/label text.

## CHECKLIST per video
- [ ] Captions ≤ 2 lines / ~6 words per line, phrase-chunked, approved style
- [ ] Visual change every ~6–9s; nothing over ~10s; gentle motion
- [ ] 3–4 natural personal touches sprinkled (not every item)
- [ ] Bracket/labels stripped from final voiceover text
